What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. We use cookies to understand how you use our calculators and to serve relevant advertising.

Cookies we use

CookieProviderPurposeDuration
pb_cookiesPropertyBrainRemembers your cookie consent choiceIndefinite (localStorage)
_gaGoogle AnalyticsDistinguishes users for analytics2 years
_ga_*Google AnalyticsSession state for GA42 years
IDE, DSIDGoogle AdSenseAdvertising personalisationUp to 13 months

Essential vs non-essential

Essential: The pb_cookies preference cookie is essential (it prevents the banner reappearing). No personal data is stored.

Non-essential: Google Analytics and AdSense cookies are non-essential. We only set them if you accept cookies via the banner. Our calculators work fully without them.

Managing cookies

You can:

  • Decline all non-essential cookies using the banner when you first visit
  • Clear your browser cookies at any time (Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data)
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Manage Google Ads: adssettings.google.com
