About PropertyBrain

What is PropertyBrain?

PropertyBrain is a free hub of UK property investment calculators. Our tools cover everything a buy-to-let investor needs: stamp duty (SDLT/LBTT/LTT), mortgage repayments, rental yield, cashflow, serviced accommodation income, HMO yield and total ROI.

All calculators work instantly in your browser with no signup, no account and no paywall. Enter your numbers, get your results.

The maths behind PropertyBrain

Our calculators use the same models as professional property software:

  • SDLT bands from HMRC (updated October 2024, including 5% additional property surcharge)
  • LBTT rates from Revenue Scotland (including 6% Additional Dwelling Supplement)
  • LTT rates from Welsh Revenue Authority
  • Mortgage formula: standard annuity calculation (P × r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1))
  • Section 24 tax: personal = gross income taxed at band rate, 20% credit on interest
  • Ltd Co: net profit (after interest) taxed at 19% corporation tax
