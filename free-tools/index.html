How to use these tools

Excel spreadsheets:

  • Yellow cells = enter your own numbers
  • White/grey cells = automatically calculated — do not overwrite
  • Compatible with Microsoft Excel 2016+ and Google Sheets
  • No macros required — formulas only
  • Print-ready formatting for presenting to lenders or investors

Legal templates (PDF):

  • Fill in the highlighted fields (property address, parties, fee amounts, dates)
  • Both parties should read the full document before signing
  • We recommend review by a qualified solicitor before use
  • All templates are for England & Wales jurisdiction
