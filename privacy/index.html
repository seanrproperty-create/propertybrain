Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Who we are

PropertyBrain.uk is owned and operated by EIGHTFINITY LTD, which also operates PropertyAlert.uk. This policy explains how we collect and use data when you visit PropertyBrain.uk.

2. Data we collect

PropertyBrain.uk does not require you to create an account or provide personal information. We collect:

  • Analytics data via Google Analytics 4 — pages visited, time on site, device type, approximate location (city level). This data is anonymised and aggregated.
  • Cookie consent preference — stored in your browser's localStorage to remember your choice.
  • Download events — when you download an Excel file, we log the event in GA4 (no personal data, just the file name and timestamp).

We do not collect: names, email addresses, property details you enter into calculators (all calculations run in your browser only — no data is sent to our servers).

3. Cookies

We use:

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) — analytics only, if you accept cookies
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense) — used to show relevant adverts, if you accept cookies

You can decline cookies on the banner that appears when you first visit. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

4. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, and object to processing. As we collect minimal data and it is anonymous, most requests are automatically satisfied.

This website is owned and operated by EIGHTFINITY LTD. For any inquiries regarding your personal data, privacy concerns, or to exercise your data rights under the UK GDPR, please contact our data protection team directly at info@eightfinity.net.

5. Third parties

We use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) and Google AdSense. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data retention

GA4 data is retained for 14 months by default. We do not store personal data ourselves.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted on this page.
